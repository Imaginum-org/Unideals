Buy and sell on campus, the easy way

Unideals is a campus-only marketplace for college students in India. Buy and sell used textbooks, gadgets, cycles and hostel essentials with verified students on your own campus. There are no listing fees and no shipping: you chat in the app, meet at a public spot on campus and pay the seller at pickup by UPI or cash.

How Unideals works

  1. Join your campus. Sign up with Google or email and pick your college. You only see listings from students on your campus.
  2. List in two minutes. Add photos, a price, the condition and a public pickup spot.
  3. Chat and agree a price. Buyers message you in the app and can make offers.
  4. Meet and pay at pickup. Check the item on campus, then pay by UPI or cash. Unideals takes no commission.
  5. Rate the deal. Ratings, badges and levels help reliable sellers stand out.

Why students use Unideals

  • Campus-only and verified: every buyer and seller is a student on your campus. Students can verify their college email to get a verified tick.
  • Zero listing fees: buying, selling and chatting are free. Optional Pro (₹99) and Pro+ (₹199) plans add more listings and boosts.
  • Safe meetups: meet at public campus spots, inspect before you pay, and report anything suspicious.
  • Girls Corner: a women-only space for clothing, beauty and personal items.

Campuses

Live at VIT Vellore, VIT Chennai, VIT Bhopal and VIT Amaravati, with more Indian campuses coming.

Frequently asked questions

Is Unideals free?
Yes. Listing, buying and chatting are free and there is no commission on sales. Optional plans and one-time boosts help listings reach more buyers.
How do I pay?
Directly to the seller when you meet on campus, by UPI or cash, after checking the item.
Is it safe?
Everyone belongs to a campus, students can verify their college email, chats are screened for abuse, and users and listings can be reported.
Can I buy from another college?
Listings are campus-only, so you trade with students you can actually meet.
